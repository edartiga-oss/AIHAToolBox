ChemSTEER
InhalationDermal
ChemSTEER generates screening-level estimates for environmental releases of and worker exposures to a chemical manufactured and used in industrial and commercial operations (i.e. workplaces). The tool also contains data and estimation methods to assess chemical use in common industrial/commercial sectors (e.g., automotive refinishing) and chemical functional uses (e.g., tackifier in adhesive). ChemSTEER does not contain methods for estimating exposures to chemicals to the general public, to consumers, or to other species in the environment.

ConsExpo Web 1.2.0
InhalationDermalOral
ConsExpo is a computer program that enables the estimation and assessment of exposure to substances from consumer products such as paint, cleaning agents and personal care products. The model is developed by the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment. Exposure via inhalation, via the skin or by oral intake can be assessed. Users choose the most appropriate scenario and fill in exposure parameters such as body weight and exposure duration. The program consists of both screening models and higher tier models for an exposure estimation.

Provides graphs showing exposure over time based on data provided. Model will calculate sensitivity analysis if further data is known and provided.

ODHMOD
Inhalation
ODHMOD™ is an Excel-based modeling tool designed to estimate oxygen concentration trends in environments where inert gas releases may create Oxygen Deficiency Hazards (ODH). The tool applies a Near Field-Far-Field (NF-FF) modeling approach to simulate the temporal evolution of oxygen concentration in two zones. It provides results as oxygen concentration (% by volume) and oxygen partial pressure (mmHg). These outputs help evaluate potential asphyxiation risks, estimate time to reach critical oxygen thresholds, and support decisions on hazard mitigation and controls.
 Refined Exposure Assessment AIHA in collaboration with Università degli Studi di Brescia
Oxygen concentration (% by volume) and oxygen partial pressure (mmHg) over time in the near field and far field, supporting estimates of time to reach critical oxygen thresholds.
Precautionary Matrix for Synthetic Nanomaterials
InhalationDermalOral
Assesses the nano-specific health and environmental risks of nanoproducts. Guides user to determine whether production or application requires nano-specific control measures. Allows for preliminary risk assessment based on the current state of knowledge, and indicates when further clarification is needed.
 Preliminary / Initial Exposure Assessment Federal Office of Public Health, Switzerland
Generates a classification category, A or B, which establishes the precautionary need. Category A (score of less than 25) suggests the need for further action is low. Category B (score of greater than 25) suggests that nano-specific action is needed.

SDM 2.0 (Structured Deterministic Model)
Inhalation
SDM 2.0 generates estimates of airborne concentrations that represent potential occupational inhalation exposure to volatile and semi-volatile chemicals and chemical mixtures, aerosols, particulates, and fibers. For gases and vapors, it provides a point estimate of the 95th percentile value of an exposure distribution that can be compared to an occupational exposure limit and allows users to evaluate the impact of different exposure control options on exposure and health risk. This tool builds upon the previously developed "Checklist Tool."
 Preliminary / Initial Exposure Assessment University of Minnesota
For gases and vapors: - Point estimate of the 95th percentile airborne concentration value of an exposure distribution, for each level of ventilation control - Exposure control category, for each level of ventilation control For aerosols, particulates, and fibers: - predicted Exposure Control Category

The Advanced REACH Tool v2.0
Inhalation
Estimates worker inhalation exposure to chemicals based on a mechanistic model with an option of a Bayesian analysis. The user can refine the exposure assessment using measurements from a built-in exposure assessment database, or from user data. The model hosts a library of 117 exposure scenarios that include 1,944 measurements
 Refined Exposure Assessment TNO
Generates an exposure estimate in mg/m3 at a selected percentile and confidence interval. Users can add Bayesian treatment to the data by either uploading their own monitoring data, or selecting analogous scenarios, in which case monitoring data affiliated with those scenarios will be used. Both the mechanistic and Bayesian results are displayed. Report can be downloaded in pdf or excel.
