Overview

Offers a tiered approach for calculating the likely exposure and subsequent risks to workers resulting from defined uses of chemicals. Calculates an RCR (risk characterization ratio) for input scenarios. It is a screening level exposure and risk assessment tool.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal and inhalation

Route Details

Dermal estimate is based on external exposure

Tool Type

Platform
Excel
Output Type
Quantitative
Statistical Treatment
Point Estimate/Deterministic
Model Principle
Conceptual (First Principle)

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Molecular weight, vapor pressure, DNEL (dermal) reference value, process category, task duration, concentration band, PPE

Outputs (What you get)

Generates airborne concentrations and external dermal exposure, along with a risk characterization ratio

Target Application

Target Population
Workers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Vapor and particulates

Strengths

Customized to assessments conducted for REACH.

Known Limitations / Notes

Use sufficiently conservative Tier-1 assumptions to provide confidence that the methodology does not wrongly dismiss risks that may later associated with concerns. Does not predict exposure to gases. Does not account for quantity.

Authors / Developers

European Center for Ecotoxicology and Toxicology of Chemicals (ECETOC)
