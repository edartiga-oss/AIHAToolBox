Overview

ConsExpo is a computer program that enables the estimation and assessment of exposure to substances from consumer products such as paint, cleaning agents and personal care products. The model is developed by the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment. Exposure via inhalation, via the skin or by oral intake can be assessed. Users choose the most appropriate scenario and fill in exposure parameters such as body weight and exposure duration. The program consists of both screening models and higher tier models for an exposure estimation.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal, inhalation, and oral

Route Details

ConsExpo calculates an external dose for each of the routes of exposure. However, there is the option to input an absorption fraction (percent). For some dermal exposure scenarios, ConsExpo offers the option of a Fixed Fraction absorption model or a Diffusion Through Skin absorption model.

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

This model has numerous parameters that can be entered based on various product use scenarios. ConsExpo uses "fact sheets" created by RIVM - these are defined consumer use scenarios available for the following products: • Air Fresheners • Cleaning products • Cosmetics • Disinfectant products • Do-It-Yourself products • Paint products • Pest control products • Children's toys These save a great deal of time and provide good default assumptions when one is working with unknowns or is new to performing such assessments.

Outputs (What you get)

Generates estimates of exposure dose to the substance in question as an event dose, daily dose or year average dose. Likewise, internal (absorbed) dose can be calculated. Provides graphs showing exposure over time based on data provided. Model will calculate sensitivity analysis if further data is known and provided.

Physical Form
Liquid, vapor, and solids

Physical Form Details

This model gives one the ability to assess solids, liquids or gasses. The model does not handle nano particles. However, there is a separate model, ConsExpo nano, that allows one to investigate potential consumer exposure to nanomaterials in consumer spray products.

Strengths

RIVM is a very reputable Dutch public health agency. They have developed ConsExpo and associated materials over the course of several decades and continue to keep it up. All of the information and results for each simulation can be saved by the user for future reference. Detailed fact sheets are available to prepopulate data for specific scenarios. This greatly facilitates the assessment process. Allows for input of parameter distributions resulting in something approaching a probabalistic assessment.

Known Limitations / Notes

The model asks for a lot of data inputs that may not be readily available. All parameters must be known - model does not work if data is missing. There are detailed documents available for the various consumer product categories called "fact sheets" that detail typical habits and practices for using those products, however most of the default values are based on the habits and practices of European consumers.
