Overview

Combines indoor fate and toxicokinetic mass balance models to simulate human exposure from chemicals used indoors and/or direct applications (e.g., dermal)

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal, inhalation, and oral

Route Details

Dermal, inhalation and oral (ingestion).

Tool Type

Platform
Web-based
Output Type
Quantitative
Statistical Treatment
Point Estimate/Deterministic
Model Principle
Conceptual (First Principle)

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

The only required properties are the identity of the chemical (e.g., CAS No.), the functional use category of the chemical and the production volume.

Outputs (What you get)

RAIDAR-ICE provides estimates of concentrations in the indoor environment; that is, air concentration and concentration of the chemical on other media. In addition, it provided exposure rates, human external and internal estimates and risk estimates for a given threshold valute.

Target Application

Target Population
Workers and consumers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Liquid, vapor, and solids

Strengths

EAS-E Suite is a comprehensive model covering a number of media and giving estimates of emissions and exposures across the lifecycle of a chemical for several scenarios.

Known Limitations / Notes

RAIDAR-ICE and the EAS-E Suite Platform take an approach different from many of the other models which will necessitate initial research to learn how to use the model properly. There is limited guidance available for using the model.
