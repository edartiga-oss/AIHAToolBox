Overview

The Nasal Pungency and Odor Detection Threshold (NPT-ODT) Estimator is a supplemental tool for the Odor Thresholds for Chemicals resource. This Excel spreadsheet estimates the concentration of a compound's odor detection or nasal pungency thresholds when no published thresholds exist.

Do not use the estimator if existing exposure limits, toxicity data, or any other toxicological information are available, or if the compound is biologically reactive or acts by a specific toxic mechanism. It is best used to make general, basic comparisons between two compounds' odor or irritation potential.

Use Cases (When to Use / Applications)

The NPT-ODT Estimator is typically used when:

  • You want to screen potential compounds for use in the workplace, or
  • You want to compare potential thresholds between compounds.

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you provide)

  • The percentile of the population for the desired threshold estimate (Row 11, Column N); for example, “50” gives the average person's threshold, while “5” gives the threshold for the fifth percentile of the population.
  • For the compound of interest, the number of each of the fragments making up the compound (starting on Row 13, Column M). For example, n-nonane has two “-CH3” fragments and seven “>CH2” fragments.

Outputs (What you get)

  • Estimated odor detection threshold (ppmv)
  • Estimated eye irritation threshold (ppmv)
  • Estimated nasal pungency threshold (ppmv)

Known Limitations / Notes

  • Recommended for use only when published odor thresholds are not available.
  • The equation estimates are based on measured thresholds from a limited number of compounds. Avoid assuming the tool provides accurate estimates for very large and complex compounds, such as those with more than 20 non-hydrogen atoms.
