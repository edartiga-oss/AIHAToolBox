Overview

Qualitatively assesses occupational health risks from ENPs so that inhalation health risks can be prioritized.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Inhalation

Tool Type

Platform
Web-based
Output Type
Qualitative/banding
Statistical Treatment
Qualitative
Model Principle
Non-conceptual

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Product characteristics (name, supplier, SDS and PIS dates, appearance, nano name, concentration, fibrous or not, num of exposed employees, usage volume, dates employees work with product) Handling/Process info (task duration and frequency, proximity to breathing zone) Work zone (size, ventilation, housekeeping frequency, maintenance inspections) Local control measures

Outputs (What you get)

Generates a table of hazard and exposure classes along with a risk score. Unique scores are calculated for time weighted and task weighted assessments.

Target Application

Target Population
Workers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Engineered nano materials

Physical Form Details

Includes agglomerates and aggregates The size of the primary particle is smaller than 100 nm and / or the specific surface area of a nanopowder is larger than 60 m2/g;

Strengths

Inputs are easy to select and determine. Can determine the impact on the exposure and risk class of changing various control measures.

Known Limitations / Notes

ENP cannot be water soluble
