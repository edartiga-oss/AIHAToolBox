Overview

Characterizes the health and exposure aspects of working with nanomaterials and determines the level of risk and controls using control banding principles.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal and inhalation

Tool Type

Platform
Access
Output Type
Qualitative/banding
Statistical Treatment
Qualitative
Model Principle
Conceptual (First Principle)

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Surface reactivity - qualitative Particle Shape Particle diameter Solubility CMR - Yes/No Dermal toxicity - Yes/No Toxicity of parent material in mg/m3 (OEL) CMR, dermal and asthmagen tox of parent - Yes/No Amount used Dustiness Number of employees exposed Frequency of operation Duration of operation

Outputs (What you get)

Generates an overall Risk Level 1-4, and associated recommended engineering controls. Indicates whether the current engineering controls (as entered by the user) need to be upgraded.

Target Application

Target Population
Workers
Product Type
Any nano-specific product
Physical Form
Engineered nano materials

Strengths

Created so that a non-expert can use it. Was tested against 5 operations that are running with controls established by experienced professionals, and the model recommendations matched actual practice very closely. - Also includes a probabilistic option

Known Limitations / Notes

-The rating scale is based on professional judgment. - Model may not work correctly for Mac users. Use the "contact us" page on the website to contact an owner to get a non-macro version of the tool
