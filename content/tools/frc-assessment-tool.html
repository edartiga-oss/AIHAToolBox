Overview

Understanding, evaluating, and selecting flame-resistant (FR) clothing can be a challenge for many health and safety (H&S) professionals. Failure to recognise the need for FR clothing leaves workers without proper protection, yet requiring it where it is not needed is expensive and can add to employee heat load and complaints. The FR Assessment Tool™ is a simple Excel spreadsheet that helps H&S professionals assess a work area and make — and document — more accurate decisions about the need for FR clothing.

Use Cases (When to Use / Applications)

The FR Assessment Tool is typically used to determine the need for FR clothing and the level of clothing protection required when handling, or working in the area of, flammable materials — and to document the decision-making process.

Note: The tool does not address FR clothing or other PPE needs related to arc-flash potential. For hazards related to electrical work, refer to NFPA 70E.

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you provide)

The main menu gives the user access to all the steps and their associated modules, guiding the user through documenting inputs, listing team members and their qualifications, and completing and documenting the assessment. An Instructions Manual, linked at the bottom of the main page, provides guidance on data entry, form completion, and a glossary for additional support in developing a business case.

Outputs (What you get)

A documented record of the decisions made for each combination of assessed task, process, operation, function, or purpose — including specification of required actions with assigned due dates, and documented FRC Assessment Team names and signatures.

Access, Requirements & Technical Notes

The FR Assessment Tool is available as a downloadable Microsoft Excel workbook. Completing the FR Assessment Tool request form on the AIHA page is required to download it.

Known Limitations / Notes

  • Familiarity with the NFPA flammability classification system is needed.
  • External access to chemical physical properties (flash point, boiling point, etc.) is required.
  • FR requirements for electrical operations (arc flash) are not covered — refer to NFPA 70E.
