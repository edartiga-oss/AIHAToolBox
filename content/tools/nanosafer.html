Overview

Determines a control band for airborne occupation exposure based on the hazards of nano materials and the processes being used.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Inhalation

Tool Type

Platform
Web-based
Output Type
Qualitative/banding
Statistical Treatment
Qualtitative
Model Principle
Non-conceptual

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Material name, main substance, information on size/shape/etc. of the particle, solubility, respirable dustiness index, hazard classification Exposure limits Handling/process info: Process name, energy level, time to perform the operation, number of times performed/day, size of room, etc.

Outputs (What you get)

Generates a report showing the control band and protection factor.

Target Application

Target Population
Workers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Engineered nano materials

Strengths

Is flexible in that you can combine different materials (with their characteristics and hazards) along with different processes to determine the level of protection needed.

Known Limitations / Notes

Software is in English, but support documents are in Danish

Authors / Developers

NanoSafer: National Research Center for the Working Environment, Denmark and the Danish Nanosafety Center
