IH SKINPERM

Refined Exposure AssessmentWorker ExposureProduct AssessmentChemical hazard

Overview

Estimates the permeability of chemicals across skin barrier. Data for over 100 substances exist in the model's database. The model offers four types of dermal absorption scenarios. Results are given in both tabular and graphical formats.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal

Route Details

Dermal estimate is based on absorbed dose.

Tool Type

Platform
Excel
Output Type
Quantitative
Statistical Treatment
Point Estimate/Deterministic
Model Principle
Conceptual (First Principle)

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Chemical name, Skin area (cm²), Thickness of stagnant air, Timing parameters (observation time), Dermal scenario: (1) Instantaneous - mass deposited (mg), Weight fraction; (2) Deposition over time - deposition rate (mg/cm²/hr), Weight fraction; (3) Dermal uptake from vapor - Air concentration (mg/m³); (4) From water solution - Concentration in water, thickness of water layer.

Outputs (What you get)

Generates a report providing total deposition (mg), fraction absorbed (%), and amount absorbed (mg), as well as a graph that displays the fraction absorbed, the fraction that volatilizes to air, and the fraction in the stratum corneum.

Target Application

Target Population
Workers and consumers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Liquid, vapor, and solids

Strengths

Includes a built-in database that allows user to select from a list of chemicals or add their own to a User's Database. Published article indicated good reliability and accuracy. The model is easy to use, and important parameters are easy to interpret. The Drolet spinner allows user to see how absorption changes over time.

Known Limitations / Notes

Modeling a mixture requires in-depth understanding of the model and the mixture chemistry. How to interpret of some of the parameters in the report is not easy. The lack of OELs for skin exposure means that existing limits (inhalation TLVs or systemic dermal DNELs) must be converted to internal dose. Molecular weight between 18 and 584 Log Kow between -3.7 and 5.49
