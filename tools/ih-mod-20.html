Overview

IHMOD™ is an Excel-based exposure modeling tool designed to estimate airborne concentrations for occupational inhalation exposure scenarios. It supports both deterministic modeling (single point estimates) and Monte Carlo simulation (parameter distributions), allowing users to evaluate exposure outcomes and uncertainty using a structured modeling approach.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Inhalation

Route Details

Inhalation exposure estimates can be derived under multiple different scenarios (e.g., well-mixed room, turbulent eddy diffusion, two-zone model)

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Room volume; air flow; worker's time in near field or far field zones; number, duration and magnitude of emission events

Outputs (What you get)

Generates modeled concentrations in air (including TWA concentration) compared with OELs (TLV, REL). Produces a graph of concentration vs. time and indicates the time at which predicted maximum concentration occurs.

Use Cases (When to Use / Applications)

IHMOD™ is typically used when:

  • A screening-level tool is not sufficient for decision-making
  • More refined, scenario-specific modeling is needed (Tier 2)
  • You want to evaluate exposure using both deterministic and probabilistic approaches
  • You need to perform "what-if" comparisons (e.g., ventilation changes, task duration changes, control options)
  • Measurement data are limited, and you need a defensible estimate to support exposure judgments, planning, or prioritization

Strengths

Built from a standard modeling approach (2-zone inhalation exposure model published in AIHA's modeling guide); offers deterministic and probabilistic modes; assesses long-term and acute exposures. Allows user to easily visualize the impact of changing various input values (i.e., for a sensitivity analysis). Some models include an option to end generation and begin purge calculation.

Known Limitations / Notes

Estimating some inputs may be difficult for novice users. Does not provide default values.
