Stoffenmanager 8.2

Preliminary / Initial Exposure AssessmentWorker ExposureChemical hazard

Overview

Stoffenmanager 8.2 is a software solution designed for managing hazardous substances, with a focus on occupational exposure and risk assessment.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Inhalation and dermal

Route Details

Inhalation risk assessment (qualitative and quantitative) and dermal risk assessment (qualitative).

Tool Type

Platform
Web-based
Output Type
Quantitative
Statistical Treatment
Point Estimate/Deterministic
Model Principle
Non-conceptual

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Product characteristics: name, supplier SDS dates and files, physical properties, location, hazard statements, expiration data, quantity stored. Components: CAS number, vapor pressure/dustiness, dilution, exposure limit values. Suppliers and job titles Processes: description, task duration/breaks, distance to source, body surface, employee number, protective equipment. Workplace: description, room volume, ventilation, cleaning, local control, well maintained, employee separation.

Outputs (What you get)

Inhalation (quantitative): a task concentration and a daily average concentration in mg/m3 per component in the product (powders/wood dust: total inhalable, stone dust: inhalable and respirable)

Target Application

Target Population
Workers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Solids (powders), liquids and abrasive activities for wood and stone

Physical Form Details

Volatile and non-volatile liquids, powders, abrasion and impact (wood/stone).

Strengths

Can assess the impact of various control measures and calculate the job daily average. Also provides inhalation and dermal prioritization qualitative risk assessments: Inhalation (qualitative): hazard class, exposure class, and risk score, hazard class for eyes and protection advice, and warnings on additional risks. Dermal (qualitative): local and systemic risk prioritization (hazard class, exposure class, and risk score),hazard class for eyes and protection advice, and warnings on additional risks.

Known Limitations / Notes

Quantitative inhalation exposure assessment model cannot be used for gas, fibers, or abrasive activities using plastic, glass, or metal and does not assess hot work techniques like welding, soldering and diesel exhaust. Inhalation and dermal qualitative control banding is not useful for substances or products without an SDS or for which hazard statements are not known or available.
