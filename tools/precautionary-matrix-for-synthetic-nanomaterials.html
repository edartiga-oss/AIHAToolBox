Precautionary Matrix for Synthetic Nanomaterials

Preliminary / Initial Exposure AssessmentWorker ExposureProduct AssessmentEnvironmental AssessmentChemical hazardPhysical hazard

Overview

Assesses the nano-specific health and environmental risks of nanoproducts. Guides user to determine whether production or application requires nano-specific control measures. Allows for preliminary risk assessment based on the current state of knowledge, and indicates when further clarification is needed.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal, inhalation, and oral

Tool Type

Platform
Web-based
Output Type
Qualitative/banding
Statistical Treatment
Qualtitative
Model Principle
Conceptual (First Principle)

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Particle size Type of nano material Redox Catalytic activity Reactivity Stability of the material Carrier model (gas, liquid, solid) Amount handled by worker or consumer Uncertainty

Outputs (What you get)

Generates a classification category, A or B, which establishes the precautionary need. Category A (score of less than 25) suggests the need for further action is low. Category B (score of greater than 25) suggests that nano-specific action is needed.

Target Application

Target Population
Workers and consumers
Product Type
Any nano-specific product
Physical Form
Engineered nano materials

Physical Form Details

Gas, liquid, or solid

Strengths

Inputting data is relatively easy and are well explained. The outputs are very descriptive and provide good information. There is a detailed user guide. Includes uncertainty calculation (probability of Class B)

Known Limitations / Notes

Data will expire in 20 minutes after last page view, so must save work periodically. Cannot select more than one exposure pathway

Authors / Developers

Precautionary Matrix: Federal Office of Public Health, Switzerland
