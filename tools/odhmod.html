ODHMOD

Refined Exposure AssessmentWorker ExposureChemical hazard

Overview

ODHMOD™ is an Excel-based modeling tool designed to estimate oxygen concentration trends in environments where inert gas releases may create Oxygen Deficiency Hazards (ODH). The tool applies a Near Field-Far-Field (NF-FF) modeling approach to simulate the temporal evolution of oxygen concentration in two zones. It provides results as oxygen concentration (% by volume) and oxygen partial pressure (mmHg). These outputs help evaluate potential asphyxiation risks, estimate time to reach critical oxygen thresholds, and support decisions on hazard mitigation and controls.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Inhalation (oxygen deficiency / displacement of breathable air)
