Overview

The COVID-19 Exposure Assessment Tool (CEAT) is a free Beta tool that estimates a group's relative exposure to SARS-CoV-2 across various activities, to show the relative importance of the factors that drive exposure. It combines mechanistic and epidemiological factors and is distributed as an editable PDF. It reports relative, not absolute, risk and is useful for comparing scenarios and the effect of combinations of controls.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Inhalation (respiratory exposure to an airborne infectious agent)

Tool Type

Platform
Editable PDF (with embedded JavaScript)
Output type
Relative exposure / risk value

Use Cases (When to Use / Applications)

Comparing the relative SARS-CoV-2 inhalation exposure of different group activities and scenarios, and evaluating how combinations of controls — ventilation, filtration, masking, distancing, group size, and duration — change relative exposure, to screen and prioritize activities during a pandemic.

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you provide)

A set of mechanistic and epidemiological factors, including aerosol generation, aerosol decay and removal, ventilation rate, room volume, air filtration, mask use and effectiveness, distance between people, group size, exposure duration, and community epidemiology (local infection prevalence, vaccination, and testing).

Outputs (What you get)

A group-wide relative exposure / relative respiratory-risk value, scaled against a high-risk reference baseline, used to compare scenarios and the relative effect of protective measures.

Known Limitations / Notes

  • Reports relative risk only, not an absolute probability of infection.
  • Released as a Beta version; the currently hosted version and continued availability should be confirmed.

Authors / Developers

Developed by Signature Science, LLC (a subsidiary of Southwest Research Institute) in collaboration with the COVID-19 International Research Team (COV-IRT).
