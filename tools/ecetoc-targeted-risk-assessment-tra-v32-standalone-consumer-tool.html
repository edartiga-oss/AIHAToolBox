ECETOC Targeted Risk Assessment (TRA) v3.2 - Standalone Consumer Tool

Product Exposure ModelingProduct AssessmentChemical hazard

Overview

Offers a tiered approach for calculating the likely exposure and subsequent risks to consumers resulting from defined uses of chemicals. Calculates an RCR (risk characterization ratio) for input scenarios. It is a screening level exposure and risk assessment tool.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal and inhalation

Route Details

Dermal estimate is based on external exposure

Tool Type

Platform
Excel
Output Type
Quantitative
Statistical Treatment
Point Estimate/Deterministic
Model Principle
Empirical

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Molecular weight and vapor pressure for inhalation exposure. Substance/product Category and sub-category. DNEL reference values for inhalation, dermal, and oral. Weight fraction (optional). Skin contact area (optional). Amount used (optional). Oral transfer factor (optional). Dermal transfer factor (optional).

Outputs (What you get)

Generates airborne concentrations and external dermal exposure, along with a risk characterization ratio

Target Application

Target Population
Consumers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Vapor and particulates

Strengths

Customized to assessments conducted for REACH. Can handle a variety of consumer products or articles such as spray products, coating, paints, adhesives, sealants, wood, paper, and more as listed in the drop down menu.

Known Limitations / Notes

Use sufficiently conservative Tier-1 assumptions to provide confidence that the methodology does not wrongly dismiss risks that may later associated with concerns.

Authors / Developers

European Center for Ecotoxicology and Toxicology of Chemicals (ECETOC)
