DRAM v1.0

Refined Exposure AssessmentWorker ExposureProduct AssessmentChemical hazard

Overview

Provides a systematic screenting evalution of the relative reisks of dermal exposure to a material. It is especially useful for the purposes of prioritization.

Exposure Pathway(s)

Dermal

Tool Type

Platform
Excel
Output Type
Qualitative/banding
Statistical Treatment
Stochastic and deterministic
Model Principle
Non-conceptual

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What you need)

Dermal hazard rating Dermal contact area Dermal loading Dermal contact frequency Dermal retention time Dermal penetration potential

Outputs (What you get)

Color coded risk matrix - Low, medium, high, very high

Target Application

Target Population
Workers and consumers
Product Type
Not documented yet
Physical Form
Liquid, vapor, and solids

Physical Form Details

This model can accommodate any physical state.

Strengths

Help screens are available to assist users in picking the most relevant rating for each exposrue determinant. The tool offers multiple ways to characterize hazard, including GHS codes. The tool is laid out in an easy to use, friendly format.

Known Limitations / Notes

This tool is designed to assist in prioritization by offering a way to compare relative risks for chemicals/tasks. Some of the inputs, such as Dermal Penetration Potential, depend on user to characterize the inputs, thus yielding an output that is highly influenced by user experience and knowledge.
